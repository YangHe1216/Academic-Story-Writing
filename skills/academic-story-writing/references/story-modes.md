# Story Contract and Story Modes

## Minimal Story Contract

Use a compact working map, not a questionnaire or immutable truth statement:

```text
STATUS: AUTHOR-CONFIRMED | PROVISIONAL
Research question:
Central thesis / scientific answer:
Why this problem matters:
Core insight (not merely the list of components):
CORE contribution(s):
ENABLING contribution(s):
SECONDARY advantages (not automatically headline claims):
Supporting evidence: claim -> proof, table, figure, source, setting
Closest inspected related work: overlap and substantive distinctions
Scope / non-negotiable assumptions / contradictory findings:
Author-locked terminology, wording, section decisions:
```

For a paragraph edit, a one-sentence thesis plus local evidence suffices. Do not ask the author to fill every field. Treat inferred claims as `PROVISIONAL` rather than author-approved. If current evidence materially conflicts with an approved story, disclose the exact conflict and propose authorized changes; do not cover it up.

## PRESERVE — Default

Keep the scientific question, central answer, and contribution hierarchy. Allowed: sharpen causal/rhetorical structure, strengthen grounded statements, reorder support, clarify scope, remove distracting secondary benefits. Forbidden unless authorized: quietly changing the thesis, promoting a tangential advantage into the core contribution, deleting negative findings, or imposing imaginary reviewer caveats. If a better story occurs to you, suggest it **outside** the requested paper text.

## DISCOVER — Explicit Story Exploration

Given methods, analyses, experiments, or rough drafts, propose 2–3 genuinely different scientific questions/framings. Compare for each:

- Central thesis and research significance.
- Distinct scientific insight and substantive novelty relative to **inspected** sources.
- Which claims are `CORE`, `ENABLING`, `SECONDARY`.
- Supporting theory/experiments, contradictions, scope, unresolved key checks.
- Expected structure of Abstract and Introduction.

Recommend the candidate with strongest evidence, coherence, and genuine contribution. It is a **candidate**, not an adopted story; do not equate enthusiasm with publication probability.

## REFRAME — Explicit Permission to Change the Story

Show an explicit before/after contract (`old thesis → new thesis`, changed evidence burden, promoted/demoted claims). Reorganize only requested manuscript files/sections. Note consequential updates to Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Theory, Experiments, Related Work, and Conclusion. Preserve author control of final framing.

## Mode Isolation

A request to *polish, edit, convert notes to paragraphs,* or *make more compelling* does **not** authorize story discovery/reframing. A request to *brainstorm better scientific stories* authorizes `DISCOVER`, not silent rewrites. A request to *review weaknesses* changes **role** to `REVIEWER`, not automatically story mode. User intent controls this separation.
